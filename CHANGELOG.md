# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release: tiers 1 to 3 of Joulesmith for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

### Materials
- Tin, lead, silver, nickel and uranium ores, with raw ores, ingots, nuggets, dusts, plates, gears, wires and storage blocks.
- Alloys: bronze, steel, invar and electrum.
- Rubber trees in forests; tap them for latex, then rubber.

### Power
- Combustion, Biofuel and Geothermal Generators, Solar Panels (three tiers), Wind Turbine, Boiler and Steam Turbine.
- Basic, Reinforced and Advanced Batteries and Energy Cables.
- Every energy bar shows the FE/t a block makes or uses.

### Transport
- Item Ducts, Fluid Pipes and Energy Cables in three tiers, set per side with the Wrench.
- Filter Cards (exact item, tag or mod; whitelist or blacklist) and routing modes.
- Pump and Advanced Pump, Fluid Tanks in three sizes, Conveyor Belt.
- Upgrade Kits upgrade cables, pipes, batteries, crates, bins, tanks and solar panels in place.

### Processing
- Electric Furnace, Crusher, Alloy Smelter, Compressor, Wire Drawer, Sawmill, Ore Washer, Fluid Mixer, Centrifuge, Circuit Assembler, Chemical Dissolver and Crystallizer.
- Ore yield climbs with each tier: 2, 3, then 4 ingots per raw ore.
- Speed, Efficiency, Bonus, Eject and Range upgrade cards.
- Fluids: sulfuric acid, coolant, biofuel, oil, liquid latex, steam, liquid XP and ore slurries.

### Automation and logic
- Block Placer, Block Breaker, Autocrafter, Fluid Placer and Drain, Mechanical User, Quarry and Mob Fighter.
- Sensors (inventory, tank, energy, time, proximity) and the Logic Controller.
- Redstone modes on every machine: ignored, on with signal, on without signal, never, pulse.

### Farming
- Planter, Harvester, Tree Farm, Bio Reactor, Sprinkler, Fertilizer Mixer, Animal Rancher, Hydroponic Bed, Mob Grinder and Auto Enchanter.

### Storage
- Crates, Bulk Bins and Fluid Tanks in three tiers.

### Gear
- Copper, bronze and steel tools and armor; steel Hammer and Excavator (3x3).
- Powered gear: Power Drill, Chainsaw, Energy Sword and the four-piece Exo-Suit, Energy Cells and the Charging Pad.
- The Modification Table and 19 modules.

### Compatibility
- JEI, Jade, The One Probe, ComputerCraft (CC: Tweaked) and Curios, all optional.
- Works with any mod that uses NeoForge energy, item and fluid capabilities.
- Balance settings in `config/joulesmith-common.toml`; recipes, loot and ore generation are data packs.
