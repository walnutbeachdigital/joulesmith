# Joulesmith

**From a coal generator to a fusion core, a storage network and a plasma cannon: one tech mod where everything plugs together.**

Joulesmith is an all-in-one tech mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge. Power, pipes, machines, farms, storage and gear share one tier ladder (Basic, Reinforced, Advanced, Elite, Ultimate), one machine screen and one set of rules, so you always know what to build next. The look follows classic Thermal-style pixel art: steel casings, bright colour-coded fronts and trims that show each block's tier.

## Features

**Power.** Burn fuel in a Combustion Generator, grow it for the Biofuel Generator, tap lava with the Geothermal Generator, or go renewable with Solar Panels and Wind Turbines. A Boiler and Steam Turbine power a whole base; later, a Fission Reactor runs on uranium and water, and a Fusion Core on deuterium and tritium. The reactor never explodes: if it runs dry, it shuts itself down. Move power with cables in five tiers, or send it wirelessly on dye-coloured channels.

**Transport.** Item Ducts, Fluid Pipes and Energy Cables connect on their own; set each side to push, pull or off with the Wrench. Filter Cards sort by item, tag or mod. Pumps, tanks and Conveyor Belts round it out, and Upgrade Kits move a working line up a tier without tearing it down.

**Processing.** Machines turn one raw ore into two, three, four and finally five ingots as you climb the tiers: crushing, washing, dissolving ore in acid and growing crystals, then melting it in plasma.

**Storage network.** Cable Drive Bays full of Item and Fluid Drives to a Network Core and reach everything from one searchable terminal, or from 64 blocks away with a Wireless Terminal. Record recipes on Patterns and let an Auto-Crafting CPU craft them, ingredients and all.

**Automation.** Block Placers and Breakers, an Autocrafter, a Quarry, a Mob Fighter, a Drone Hub whose drones fetch drops and harvest crops, Sensors and a Logic Controller that can say "if the battery is below 20%, start the generators."

**Farming.** Planters, Harvesters, Tree Farms, Sprinklers, Hydroponic Beds, an Animal Rancher, a Mob Grinder that stores XP as a fluid, a Mob Duplicator, and an Auto Enchanter to spend the XP.

**Gear.** Copper, bronze and steel tools and armor, then FE-powered gear: a Power Drill, Chainsaw, Energy Sword, Omni-Tool, Railgun and Plasma Cannon, and the Exo-Suit in three tiers with up to six modules each, from Fortune and Night Vision to a Jetpack and creative-style Flight.

## Compatibility

Joulesmith uses NeoForge's standard FE, item and fluid capabilities, so it works alongside other tech mods. Optional support for JEI, Jade, The One Probe, CC: Tweaked and Curios. Balance settings live in `config/joulesmith-common.toml`; recipes and ore generation can be changed with data packs.

## Languages

English, Simplified Chinese, Russian, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese (JEI info pages and longer descriptions are English for now).

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1. Install on both client and server.
